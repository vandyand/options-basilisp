Live-versus-historical market-data parity

The data foundation is now strong enough to begin serious, leakage-resistant model research. The key achievement is not a claim of alpha: it is having named data contracts whose historical replay is known to match, or closely match, the live representation used at decision time.

Vendor findings

ThetaDataUS options, indexes, and stocks
Primary options source

01 Options quotes, trades, and indexes Hard

Exact-contract option quote and trade comparisons established robust live-to-history parity on deterministic samples across SPY, SPX, SPXW, VIX, and VIXW. A completed all-chain 15-minute discovery seed then measured 19.09 GiB of raw NDJSON across those roots: this is strong evidence for the information available, and equally strong evidence that indiscriminate full-session archival is not yet an approved storage plan.

  • Option NBBO quotes: admitted as hard parity. Preserve option contract identity, event timestamp, endpoint parameters, and capture receipt/watermark.
  • Option trades: admitted as hard parity. Trade/quote relationships must be constructed locally under one explicit event-time rule.
  • Availability check: small read-only SPY tick probes returned quote and trade data for 2016–2025; equivalent 2015 requests were entitlement-denied. This verifies a ten-year usable history boundary, not retrospective live-parity evidence for every historical session.
  • Index prices: admitted as hard parity. The feed is change-only, so live and historical code must use the same causal carry-forward implementation. Six sampled index symbols returned all four storage-study windows; VIXN returned vendor HTTP 472 for July 10 and is not silently treated as available in that seed.
  • Vendor IV and Greeks: not admitted as raw inputs. Exact snapshot/history equality was not established because vendor calculations can use different underlying timestamps. Compute Greeks deterministically from parity-proven option inputs instead.
  • Open interest: excluded from the intraminute corpus. It is a prior-session daily state, not intraminute flow.

02 Stocks: live feed and retained history differ Quarantined

Live stock data is NQB (Nasdaq Basic), while long-horizon history resolves to distinct UTP/CTA consolidated-tape provenance. Same-session NQB trade checks were stronger than quote checks, but neither establishes a two-year same-feed historical replay. Treat SPY as a stock/ETF in this contract, not an index. The apparent stock feed difference is a data-contract issue, not a feature-engineering nuisance that should be normalized away without proof.

DatabentoCME Globex futures and a derived US equity composite

01 GLBX.MDP3: hard completed bars, L1, and trades Hard

GLBX is Databento’s CME Globex MDP 3.0 dataset, covering CME Group futures and related products across CME, CBOT, NYMEX, and COMEX. The live entitlement supports top-of-book prices, trades, and completed bars; it does not currently authorize deeper-book data.

  • Completed bars: the July 16 six-root capture (ES.FUT, NQ.FUT, RTY.FUT, ZN.FUT, ZB.FUT, SR3.FUT) matched exactly: 2,265 complete one-second bars and 241 complete one-minute bars. Partial start-boundary bars were excluded correctly.
  • MBP-1: 742,277 of 742,279 captured events matched exactly after excluding transport/receipt fields (ts_recv, ts_out, ts_index, ts_in_delta). One SR3 calendar-spread event differed only in side, and one live event was at the end-exclusive historical boundary. This is hard parity with bounded residual evidence.
  • Trades: every economic trade field matched. Sixteen records differed only in Databento F_LAST, a packet/batch-end flag, and one live record was on the end boundary. Only F_LAST may be masked; all other quality flags remain data.

02 EQUS Mini: hard completed bars, L1, and trades Hard

EQUS Mini is a Databento-derived, anonymized top-of-book composite from alternative trading systems and regulated US market sources. It is not a consolidated SIP/NBBO feed and not a direct-exchange feed, so that identity must remain in every feature and model artifact.

  • Completed bars: 3,077 complete one-second bars and 135 complete one-minute bars matched exactly across a 15-symbol equity basket.
  • MBP-1: 617,582/618,214 matched after transport fields were excluded: 99.8978%, with 632 live-only and zero historical-only events.
  • Trades: 8,355/8,360 matched: 99.9402%, with five live-only and zero historical-only events.
Databento archive-finalization rule. Current-day historical metadata explicitly warns that it is not finalized and can overestimate costs/sizes. Intraday comparisons are valuable diagnostics, but every raw seed session must be rechecked after archival finalization before it is promoted in a training cohort.
Alpaca IEXSingle-exchange US equity view
Not a consolidated tape

01 Causal quote state is exact from 10 ms upward Hard state

Alpaca’s IEX feed is a real live exchange feed, but it is not SIP/NBBO or the full consolidated US equity market. It can still carry independent signal, provided it is modeled and evaluated as IEX-only data rather than silently substituted for a consolidated tape.

  • Availability check: the authorized IEX historical endpoint returned dense SPY opening-minute quote data in each 2022–2025 probe (2,438–5,918 records), but the same 2021 probe had one record and 2020 had none. The table therefore claims only the verified dense four-year horizon; it does not treat the vendor’s broader advertised archive as equivalent evidence.
  • Trades: two expanded-window captures across 20 symbols matched 4,362/4,362 events. This is a strong provisional candidate, but needs multi-day and older-history confirmation before broader admission.
  • Raw quotes: not strict event parity. 30 live-only quote events appeared out of 246,955, with zero historical-only records.
  • Causal quote state: all 1,076,079 shared 10 ms buckets were exact, therefore the tested 100 ms, 500 ms, one-second, and one-minute states were exact as well. The admitted representation is causal last-quote state at 10 ms or coarser.
Other venuesFX and crypto context sources
Each contract assessed separately

01 OANDA FX: completed candles, not raw stream parity Candidate

OANDA’s pricing stream is capped at four updates per second and independently windowed, so it is not a faithful raw-event replay source. The valid candidate is completed bid/ask S5 or M1 candles using the same declared completion rule. A liquid-session capture across seven major FX pairs passed 1,315/1,315 completed S5/M1 candle comparisons. This is a promising provisional hard-parity candidate, pending repeated liquid-session and older-history validation.

02 Binance USD-M: delayed REST-confirmed candles only Candidate

Public WebSocket access was not usable from the available networks, and zero-lag REST candles continued to revise after nominal close. With an explicit ten-second post-close completion watermark, all 21/21 tested one-minute candles across BTCUSDT, ETHUSDT, and SOLUSDT matched later REST history exactly. It remains provisional until multi-session validation; do not call this WebSocket parity.

03 Bitunix: streamed one-minute candles fail parity Rejected

In the BTCUSDT/ETHUSDT/SOLUSDT study, only 4/18 completed WebSocket candles matched REST history exactly. Fourteen had volume differences, five also had different closes, and some had high/low differences. It is excluded from the hard-parity corpus for streamed candle features. A separate future study could test confirmed live REST polling, but it cannot inherit this result.

Method and boundary conditions

The question being testedFor a precisely declared data class, can live observations and later historical observations be replayed into the same model representation without unexplained differences?
ControlWhy it mattersApplied rule
Same source contractMatching prices alone does not prove matching feed semantics.Record vendor, dataset/feed, endpoint/schema, symbology, symbol/contract selection, and entitlement.
Event versus stateA missing raw event can be irrelevant to a stable causal state, while a state comparison can hide event-order differences.Declare whether the admitted representation is raw event, causal quote state, or completed bar. Never switch after seeing results.
Boundary handlingSubscriptions begin mid-interval and historical request ends are commonly exclusive.Exclude partial start bars; label end-boundary records rather than miscalling them vendor mismatches.
Transport fieldsReceipt/ingest latency fields are not stable market-data fields across a live connection and historical replay.Exclude only the named transport fields where documented. Do not use their removal to hide price, size, side, sequence, or quality-flag changes.
Cost controlDatabento charges usage-based historical data outside included entitlement ranges.Call metadata cost and billable-size endpoints first; block requests above the approved ceiling. Use record limits instead of killing a large stream mid-file.
Final archiveCurrent-day data can still be finalized or corrected after the live session.Treat intraday results as diagnostic and run the same comparison on the finalized archive for every raw seed session.

What this unlocks, and what still has to happen

Ready now: build a source-aware, causal feature pipeline and run model research on the admitted contracts. Preserve provider and representation identity throughout training, backtest, shadow inference, and review. Do not concatenate heterogeneous feeds as though they were one tape.
Immediate data gates
  • 1. Re-run GLBX and EQUS raw comparisons after Databento finalizes the July 16 archive, then retain the receipt and residual report beside each seed session.
  • 2. Collect multiple sessions, including different volatility and liquidity regimes, before promoting provisional candidates or selecting a production feature set.
  • 3. Keep every historical Databento download behind the cost preflight. One-year included raw windows are sufficient for an initial seed corpus; older raw history requires an explicit budget decision.
  • 4. Preserve ThetaData stock quarantine unless a genuine long-horizon same-feed contract is found. Do not train on UTP/CTA and infer on NQB under a shared “stock” label.
Model-research gates
  • 1. Start from event-native inputs. Search a declared causal aggregation grammar only after the raw receipt and representation contract are fixed.
  • 2. Use chronological, purged walk-forward splits and evaluate after realistic execution costs. High-frequency rows are not independent market regimes.
  • 3. Run group-wise conditional-utility studies: options alone, indexes/context, GLBX macro/rates context, EQUS Mini context, and IEX-only features. An additional feed is retained only if it adds out-of-sample value.
  • 4. Shadow the best candidates live before relying on results. A parity-qualified backtest is a prerequisite for a credible paper-trading result, not the result itself.
